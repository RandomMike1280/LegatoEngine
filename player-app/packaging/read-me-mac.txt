LegatoPlayer for Mac (Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 10.15 or later)
=========================================================================

Plays LegatoEngine scripts (.apno files) on Roblox pianos by pressing the keys for you.
It only plays scripts; turning songs into scripts needs the full LegatoEngine app.

Installing
1. Drag "LegatoPlayer" into your Applications folder.
2. Open it. If macOS says it can't check the app for malicious software, open
   System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click "Open Anyway".
   (The app isn't notarized by Apple, so macOS asks once.)

Letting it press keys
The first time you press Play, macOS asks you to allow LegatoPlayer in
System Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility. Turn it on, then press Play again.
If an older copy was in that list, remove it with the minus button and add this one.

Your scripts
Put .apno files in Music > LegatoEngine (folders inside it are fine; "Show folder" opens it),
choose another folder with "Change folder...", or drop a script on the window.

Playing
1. In Roblox, sit at a piano.
2. In the player, pick a script and press Play: it brings Roblox to the front and counts
   down. Or, with Roblox in front, press F6 to start or pause and F7 to stop.
   On a MacBook keyboard, hold fn with the F-key. You can choose other hotkeys.
3. Click the piano roll to start partway through a song.

Options
- Speed: slower makes fast songs easier for the game to keep up with.
- Keyboard-possible mode: plays only chords a real keyboard can press at once
  (no capital and lowercase keys together, no Ctrl keys with normal ones).
- Test mode: runs through the song without pressing anything.
